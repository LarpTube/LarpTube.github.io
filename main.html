About LarpTube

LarpTube is an experimental privacy-first video platform.

Our goal is to make video discovery useful without unnecessary tracking, invasive profiling or behavioural advertising.

This prototype uses sample listings. Real accounts, uploads, and a private video backend are not connected yet.
